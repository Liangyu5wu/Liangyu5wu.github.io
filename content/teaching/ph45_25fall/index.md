**This class is the final course in a sequence of calculus-based, introductory physics courses for scientists and engineers focused on thermodynamics and optics.**

## Class Schedule

| Week | Monday                                                                    | Wednesday                                                  |
|------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1    | Temperature, Zeroth Law, Thermal Expansion   | Heat, Calorimetry, Phase Changes, Transfer Mechanisms |
| 2    | Thermal Radiation, Ideal Gases           | Kinetic Theory of Thermodynamics    |
| 3    | Thermodynamic Systems & The First Law              | Thermodynamic Processes                |
| 4    | Reversibility & Heat Engines                      | Refrigerators, Heat Pumps, The Second Law |
| 5    | The Carnot Cycle                                       | Entropy                               |
| 6    | Light: Propagation, Reflection, & Refraction      | Refraction & Dispersion, Plane Mirrors |
| 7    | Spherical Mirrors                                       | Refraction & Thin Lenses               |
| 8    | Compound Lenses and the Eye                    | Mechanical Waves               |
| 9    | Huygen's Principle, Multi-Slit Interference | Thin Film Interference                  |
| 10   | Single and Double Slit Diffraction                 | Diffraction Gratings & Diffraction Limited Optics |
